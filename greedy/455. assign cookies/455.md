# num. name 
> **Difficulty:**   easy
> **Topics:** sorting, arrays
> **Pattern:** two ptrs, greedy
> **Link:**https://leetcode.com/problems/assign-cookies/description/

---

##  Approach

### Key Insight
Satisfy the easiest requirement with the smallest resource that works.

### Algorithm

1. sort both s and g arrays
2. match the smallest g with the smallest s that can satisfy g[i]<=s[j], count all such matches
3. the final count is our ans

### Why it works
s and g are both sorted in ascending order
if there's an element in g that no s can match, then the rest of the eleemnts in g would also not be matched.
otherwise, we're just allocating smaller resources (cookies) to clinets (children) with smaller needs.
